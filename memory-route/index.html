1. Introduction

Small Digits respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Memory Route handles information when you use our Android application.

2. Information Stored

Memory Route stores gameplay information locally on your device, including game progress, scores, daily challenge activity, and sound or game preferences. This helps the application remember your progress and settings.

3. Personal Information

Memory Route does not require an account or personal information to play. The application is designed to work offline.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

Gameplay information and preferences are stored locally on your device. You may remove this information by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling it, subject to device backup settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Memory Route is designed without advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, or external account services. The developer does not intentionally transmit or share locally stored gameplay information with third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

Memory Route does not require children or other users to provide personal information. We aim to minimize information collection for all players.

7. Data Security

We aim to protect user privacy by minimizing information collection and keeping gameplay information on the device. No storage method is completely secure.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its privacy practices change. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.
